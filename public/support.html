Support

Questions before you buy, or help with a PC we built. A real person on our team reads every message.

Get in touch

Email hello@framefoundrypc.com. We reply within one business day, usually much sooner. For help with a PC we built, include your order number and a short description or phone video of the problem.

Want a price? The quote form is the fastest way.

Common questions

Why not just buy a prebuilt?

You can, and sometimes it is the better deal - we will tell you if so. What you get here: the exact parts you want, every price shown, a quiet and tidy build, delivery and setup by hand, and local help when something goes wrong instead of shipping the PC back to a warehouse.

Can I see you in person or pick up my PC?

We work from a home workshop with no storefront, so we come to you instead: delivery, setup and upgrades all happen at your home. If you would rather meet before ordering, we can set up a video call.

How long does a build take?

Usually 5 to 10 days from deposit to delivery, mostly waiting for parts. Need it by a date? Put it on the quote form.

What if prices drop after I order?

Parts are bought the day your deposit clears, at that day's price, so the quote is the price. If the price we actually pay is lower than quoted, the difference comes off your balance.

Do you sell monitors, keyboards and headsets?

Yes, at the same cost plus 5%, and we will set them up with the PC.

Can you upgrade a PC you did not build?

Yes. Tell us what is in it (or send a photo of the inside) and we will say what is worth upgrading. See upgrades.

Do you ship?

No. Every PC is delivered by hand within our service area. See delivery prices.

Something is wrong with my PC

Email us. Most problems are solved remotely in minutes. If it is hardware, it is covered by the warranty for a year and we come to you.

How do I pay?

Card or bank transfer. A 30% deposit confirms the order and the rest is due on delivery. Details are in the terms.
