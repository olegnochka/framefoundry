Is 16GB of RAM enough for gaming in 2026?

For today's popular games, 16GB is enough. 32GB is worth it if you stream, edit video or keep a lot open while you play. What it costs right now and how to decide.

3 min read memory upgrades buying advice

Short answer: for playing today's popular games, yes. 16GB of fast DDR5 memory runs games like Fortnite, Valorant, Minecraft, Roblox, Rocket League and Marvel Rivals smoothly, and it is what our entry build ships with. 32GB starts to pay off when the PC does more than one thing at a time. Here is how to tell which camp you are in.

When 16GB is enough

  • You mostly play one game at a time, with Discord and maybe a browser tab or two open.
  • You play at 1080p or 1440p on high settings.
  • The PC is a first gaming PC, or the budget is tight and the money is better spent on the graphics card.

When 32GB is worth it

  • You stream or record while playing - the game, the streaming software and the browser all want memory at once.
  • You edit video or photos, or run creative apps alongside games.
  • You keep a lot open: dozens of browser tabs, chat, music and launchers in the background.
  • You plan to keep the PC for many years and would rather not open it up again.

That is why our Performance and Quiet Creator builds come with 32GB: they are built for high frame rates with streaming on top, and for creative work.

What it costs right now

Memory is unusually expensive this year - a 32GB DDR5-6000 kit averages around $630 on PCPartPicker's trend chart, roughly five times its mid-2025 level. In our builds today:

So going from 16GB to 32GB adds about $230 at the moment. That is the first upgrade we suggest on the Mainstream build, and it is an easy one to do later.

A few things that matter more than the number

  • Two sticks, not one. Memory runs in dual channel with two matching sticks, which is noticeably faster than a single stick of the same total size.
  • Speed and profile. DDR5-6000 is the sweet spot for current AMD Ryzen processors, but only if its EXPO or XMP profile is turned on in the BIOS - otherwise it runs slower. We set it on every build.
  • Leave room to grow. A board with four memory slots lets you add a second matching pair later instead of replacing what you have.

If you already have a PC

If your games stutter while other apps are open, and Task Manager shows memory near full while you play, more memory is one of the cheapest upgrades that makes a difference.
