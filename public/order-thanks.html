What happens next

  1. Today: we order your parts and email you within 24 hours to confirm the build and pick a delivery day.
  2. Build and test: assembly, BIOS and memory settings, Windows, drivers, a stress test and a quality checklist, with photos sent to you.
  3. Delivery: we bring it to you and make sure it works. If you paid a deposit, the balance is due then - you pay it by tapping your card or phone, and we use your order number to match it to your deposit.

If a part sells out before we can buy it, we contact you with options first - we never swap a part without your OK. Questions any time: hello@framefoundrypc.com.
