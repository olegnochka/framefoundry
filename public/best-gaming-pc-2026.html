Common questions

Is $1,999 enough for 1440p gaming?

For today's popular games, yes: the Mainstream build runs them comfortably at 1440p and smoothly at 1080p on high settings. For the newest, heaviest games at high frame rates, the Performance build is the better match.

16GB or 32GB of memory?

16GB plays games fine. 32GB helps when you stream, keep Discord and a browser open while playing, or edit video. Memory prices are high right now, so we start the entry build at 16GB and suggest 32GB as the first upgrade.

Do these prices include tax and delivery?

The prices are before sales tax, which is added at checkout for your delivery address. Delivery is free within 10 miles of Canoga Park; farther areas are listed on the services page.

Will the prices change?

We check every part price daily. If a part moves more than 5%, the build price is updated. Once you pay, your price is locked - if a part rises afterwards, we absorb it. This month's price moves are in our price report.
