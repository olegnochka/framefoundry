Upgrades and refreshes

With memory and graphics card prices this high, upgrading the right parts often beats buying a new PC. We look at what you have, tell you what is worth keeping, and do the work at your home - your PC never has to leave the house.

  • Graphics card, memory, storage, cooler or power supply swaps
  • Moving an existing PC into a new, quieter or better-cooled case
  • Clean-up: dust, fresh thermal paste, fan tuning, Windows reinstall

Labor is quoted up front for each job, before any work starts, and parts are at cost plus 5% like everything else. Ask for an upgrade quote and list what is in your PC now.

Tune-ups and Frame Foundry Care

Dust and dried-out thermal paste are what make a good PC loud and slow after a year or two. A yearly tune-up keeps it running like the day it arrived.

Tune-up

$79 per visit, when you need it

  • Dust cleaned out of the case, fans, filters and heatsinks
  • Fresh thermal paste on the processor
  • BIOS, driver and Windows updates
  • Fan curves checked, then a stress test with temperatures and drive health, and a short report for you
  • About an hour, at your home - for any desktop PC, not only ones we built

Frame Foundry Care

$99 a year, renews automatically - cancel any time

  • One tune-up every plan year, booked whenever suits you (a $79 value)
  • Free repair labor when a part fails in a PC we built - keeps our 1-year labor warranty going for as long as you stay on the plan, including handling the manufacturer claim for you. Other PCs: 20% off repair labor
  • Priority replies - the same business day when you write by 3 PM, Monday to Friday
  • Up to 3 remote help sessions a year for hardware or performance questions
  • If we have a compatible part in stock, we swap it in while a manufacturer claim runs (when available, not guaranteed)

Visits within 10 miles are included; farther away, the delivery price below applies per visit. Graphics card repasting is not included (it can affect the card's warranty) and is quoted separately. Care renews every year at the then-current price (now $99 plus any sales tax) until you cancel. We email you about 30 days before each renewal, and you can cancel online in a minute, or by emailing us. Full terms are in the terms of sale.
