How it works

  1. Tell us about it. Fill in the quote form: budget, games, monitor, noise, looks and deadline.
  2. Get an itemized quote within 24 hours. Every part, its price, a PCPartPicker link to check it, the build fee, setup and delivery. Valid for 72 hours.
  3. Pay a 30% deposit. We order parts the day it clears. Nothing changes without your approval.
  4. We build and test it. Assembly, BIOS and memory settings, Windows, drivers, a stress test and a quality checklist, with photos sent to you.
  5. Delivered and set up. We bring it to your home, pay the balance on handoff, and (with setup) connect it, sign in and set parental controls if you want them.
