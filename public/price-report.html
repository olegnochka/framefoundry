How we track prices

  • Every morning we check each part's lowest price on PCPartPicker, or Newegg's own price where Newegg sells it directly. These are national US online prices - the prices we pay to buy parts for PCs we build in Los Angeles.
  • When a part moves more than 5% from the price on our builds page, we re-price the builds that use it. Your price is locked once you pay - if a part rises afterwards, we absorb it (Terms of Sale).
  • The raw data is public to view: price-history.csv has every daily check since we started. It is ours - see how you may use it.
