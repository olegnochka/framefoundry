Short answer: November split our parts list in two. Between October 31 and November 25, all three graphics cards in our builds got more expensive, by 7% to 15%, and so did the Ryzen 7 9800X3D. Almost everything else held steady or got cheaper - memory, both SSDs (solid state drives), the quiet build's cooler and case most of all. Because the graphics card is the biggest line in every build, the net effect was small: the Mainstream and Performance builds are each up $100 since November 1, and the Quiet Creator is back where it started the month. Below is the part-by-part list and the swings in between. The full daily table and the raw log, a public CSV (comma-separated values) file, are on the [November price report](price-report.html).

## How the log works

Same as in [October](blog-october-price-notes.html): every morning we look up each part's lowest in-stock price on PCPartPicker, or Newegg's own price where Newegg sells the part directly. These are national US online prices, before sales tax. Two habits matter for reading November's numbers:

- **We do not count promo-code prices.** Black Friday month was full of them. On many days PCPartPicker listed the Ryzen 5 9600X at $169.00, but that was Newegg's price after a $50 promo code on a $219.00 sticker, so the log kept the lowest price without a code, $171.95 to $178.99.
- **When a part sells out, the log follows the part in the build.** In November that happened once: the 16GB memory kit. And on November 17 the automated check was cut off partway through, so several parts have no price that day. We left them blank rather than guess.

Three things stand out.

**Graphics cards kept climbing, in steps.** The RX 9060 XT bounced between $649.99 and $729.99 in the first week of the month as the Amazon listing came and went, then settled at $749.99 from November 5 and has not moved since. The RX 9070 XT went to $999.99 on November 4 and $1,049.99 on November 10. The RTX 5070 Ti peaked at $1,500.00 on November 3, when Best Buy was the only store with it in stock; it dipped to $1,399.00 at Amazon for a single day, November 15, and has drifted down to $1,449.99 since. Our [fall price post](blog-graphics-card-and-memory-prices-fall-2026.html) explains why graphics card prices are up this year; November did not reverse it.

**Memory was the wild one.** The 32GB Corsair kit sat at $499.99 for half the month, then jumped to $586.99 on November 17 when Corsair's own store was the only listing, held there three days, and fell to $474.99 on November 20, where it still is. The 16GB kit in the Mainstream build sold out on November 7; we moved to a TeamGroup kit at $279.99, and when that sold out on November 24, to a Patriot Viper Venom kit at $249.99 - the cheapest 16GB kit we have logged since we started.

**The quiet build's parts went on sale.** The Fractal North case dropped to $116.24 on November 20, went back to $154.99 the next day, then dropped again to $115.99 on November 23. The Dark Rock Pro 5 cooler went to $69.90 on November 19, and the 1TB SSD to $139.99 the same day. Those cuts are what kept the Quiet Creator flat despite its pricier graphics card and processor.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mainstream | $2,099 | $2,099 | $2,199 | $2,199 |
| Performance | $2,699 | $2,699 | $2,899 | $2,799 |
| Quiet Creator | $3,899 | $3,899 | $4,099 | $3,899 |

The Mainstream build is up $100, almost all of it the RX 9060 XT. The Performance build is up $100 too: the RX 9070 XT's $130 rise, partly offset by cheaper memory and storage. It touched $2,899 on November 18 and 19, the days of the memory spike. The Quiet Creator spent most of the month at $3,999 to $4,099 and came back to $3,899 on November 20, once the memory, case and cooler prices fell.

## What this means if you are buying

- **A quote is good for 72 hours.** After that, our [terms](terms.html) say we re-price before taking a deposit, because a part can move 10% or more overnight - November had several of those days.
- **Once you pay, your price is locked.** If a part rises before we buy it, we absorb it. If a part goes out of stock or changes price before we can buy it, we contact you with options: wait, a named alternative with its price, or cancel.
- **Upgrading instead?** Memory and SSDs were cheaper at the end of November than at the start, which makes a storage or memory upgrade for an existing PC a better deal than it was a month ago - see [upgrades](services.html).
